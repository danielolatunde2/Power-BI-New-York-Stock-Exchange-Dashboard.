# Power-BI-New-York-Stock-Exchange-Dashboard.
This project shows the the total capital expenditure, net income trends, cash flow trends and liabilities trends of New York using KPI and interactive charts.

## Project Highlights
- this project covers reporting periods 2012-2016 Analysis records of New York Stock Exchanges.
- It show over views of their Total Expenditures of $2,229,687,825,000.
- It explain the statistical Trends of various Net cash flows
-  The Net Income trends was statistically explained.

## Key Insights
- Year 2014 Generated the highest percentage of Tax income of #27.85%.
- Year 2015 recorded highest sum of Capital Surplus of #2,462,284,483,000.
-  KPI Reveals the Total income Tax  to be #1,192,184,146,00.
-  The charts reveals 2015 to be the year that generated highest Sum of Gross profit of 25.17%.

 ## Tools and Skills
-  Power Query.
-  Power BI
-  slicer (interractive filters).
-  Dashboard Designs.
-  Visuals and Analytical Storytelling.

 ## Dashboards Features
-  Sum of Gross Profit By Year.
-  Income Tax By Year.
-  Different Net Cash Flow trends By Year
-  Different Net Income Trends By Year.

 ## Recommendations
-  Ensure sustainability of gross profits.
- Cash inflow should be strengthened by monitoring financial inflow and controlling Capital Expenditure.
- Investment Decision should be strengthened. 
- Financial performance should be monitor such as profitability,, liability, and investment return in other to identify financial weakness early in other to make informed decision needed.
